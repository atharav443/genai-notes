Watch the VAE train

A real 1-D VAE, trained for 12,000 steps, every frame recorded. Data is two clusters at ±2. Latent is 1-dimensional.
real data generated (z ~ N(0,1) → decoder) decoder map x̂(z) prior N(0,1)

What to watch, and why

  • panel 1Data space. Grey = the real distribution (two clusters). Teal = what the model generates. Early on it's a formless blob; watch it split into two clusters. That's the decoder learning the data — fed only by the reconstruction gradient.
  • panel 2Latent space. Each small curve is one data point's posterior q(z|x). Watch them spread apart (to stay informative → reconstruction wins) while staying inside the prior's hump (→ KL wins). Their final arrangement is the truce between the two forces.
  • panel 3The decoder. This is the generative function x̂(z): a knob at position z renders this data value. Watch it bend into a step — latent left of centre → cluster −2, right of centre → cluster +2.
  • readoutNote recon falls while KL rises. The encoder is deliberately paying KL to become informative. That trade is the whole algorithm.
