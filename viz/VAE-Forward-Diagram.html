How VAE training works — end to end

One pass = one forward sweep, one loss, one backward sweep, one update. Then repeat.  (page 1 of 2)
Reading it: the data x goes into the encoder, which emits the parameters of a Gaussian over the code (μ and logσ²) — not a code. A fresh noise sample ε is combined with them to produce z. The decoder maps z to a reconstruction x̂. The two loss terms are computed from different inputs (x & x̂ for reconstruction; μ & σ for KL) and summed. One backward pass then routes ∇θ into the decoder alone and ∇φ into the encoder from both terms.
