The backward pass — every local derivative

Read top → bottom: the gradient is peeled backwards through the graph, one node at a time. Each edge shows its local derivative; each box shows what has accumulated.
The two terminals are different, and that is the whole story. θ finishes early — the reconstruction gradient reaches it and stops, because KL contains no θ. φ finishes last — it must sum a reconstruction contribution that has travelled all the way back through z, plus a direct KL contribution.
