Interactive — the ELBO trade-off & posterior collapse

Reconstruction wants a tight, informative \(q_\phi(z|x)\). The KL term pulls it onto the prior \(p(z)=\mathcal N(0,I)\). Push \(\beta\) too high and \(\mu_\phi\to0,\ \sigma_\phi\to1\) for every \(x\) — the decoder can no longer tell inputs apart.
