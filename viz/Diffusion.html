Interactive — forward noising & reverse (DDIM, σ=0)

The forward process is fixed, not learned: \(x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon_t\). Scrub \(t\) to destroy the image, then run the reverse. With an oracle denoiser and \(\sigma_t=0\), the reverse is fully deterministic — exactly the DDIM update on page 110.
