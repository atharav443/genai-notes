Probability, from first principles

Seven essays in the mnemonic medium covering the ground every other page here assumes: counting, conditioning, distributions, expectation, densities, joint distributions, and the limit theorems. One chapter of Blitzstein & Hwang per essay, each with recall cards and a review deck.

Probability · V

Densities, bell curves, and the noise we add on purpose

A density is not a probability → the normal, and why there is only one → memorylessness → universality of the uniform → reparameterization, written as μ + σε. Chapter 5. 32 cards.

Probability · VI

Joint distributions, the Jacobian, and conditioning on a variable

Marginals, conditionals and slices → covariance, and uncorrelated-but-dependent → the multivariate normal → change of variables → the tower property that produces the ELBO. Chapters 7–9. 34 cards.
